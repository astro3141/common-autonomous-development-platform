## 1. Position and thinness rule

The withdrawn Conductor integration (PRs 199–204) failed by constitutionalizing an orchestrator's runtime; every Conductor concept demanded a CADP counterpart (Design D0). This document is its replacement at a fraction of that size, and the size bound is structural, not stylistic: per Spec v0.5 §4.1, mutation intents arriving as `EffectRequestV1` through an effect client is the **whole** mandatory integration between any workflow plane and CADP. Everything below specifies that boundary and nothing behind it. A Conductor workflow, a cron job, and a human at a terminal are equal citizens at this boundary (Spec v0.5 §4.2); nothing here is Conductor-specific except measured facts grounding refusals (§5, §6).

## 2. Scope fence (non-goals)

Per Design D7-2 and D7-3, this document contains, normatively:

- **no orchestrator task states** — how a Conductor (or Temporal, or cron) run represents progress is its business;
- **no retry semantics** — the orchestrator may retry its own steps freely; the *effect* retry discipline is the Authority Plane's (Spec v0.5 §4.4: no blind retry), stated there, not here;
- **no checkpoint model** — orchestrator durability is never authority (Spec v0.5 §4.1);
- **no DAG vocabulary** and **no Conductor YAML schema** — workflow shape never crosses the boundary.

Orchestrator internals appear below **only** as measured facts grounding refusals (Design preamble; §5, §6). If a future revision of this document states any of the above normatively, that is a regression to the withdrawn approach and grounds for REQUEST_CHANGES on its own.

## 3. The external-candidate effect client

### 3.1 Measured gap (P0)

v0.4 exposes no surface for an externally-produced candidate to enter governance. The live surfaces — `cadp/live/mcpServer.ts` (`cadp_work_start`) and `cadp/live/ctl.ts` — only start the internal Temporal pipeline: `cadpWork` (`cadp/product/workflows.ts`) runs its own `implementCandidate` / `verifyCandidate` / `reviewCandidate` activities. A candidate produced anywhere else (a Conductor workflow's working tree, a cron job, a human clone) has no path to the governed effects `GIT_PUSH`, `PR_CREATE`, `PR_MERGE` except by not being governed at all. This section closes that gap with the minimal caller surface.

### 3.2 Caller surface — no new API, no caller-contract delta

The client is the existing Kernel API of TD v0.4 §12, unchanged: `put_blob`, `allocate_effect_id`, `seal_effect_request`, `submit_evidence`, `assemble_admission_input`, `evaluate`, `admit_and_dispatch`, `get_effect_state`, `request_reconcile`, plus the reads. An external effect proposer authenticates as its own registered principal (mTLS/SPIFFE or, for a human terminal, the IdP path) with the **workflow/orchestrator caller class reach** of the TD v0.4 §12 matrix. No new endpoint, no new record kind — and, correcting the prior draft, **no caller-contract (method-reach) delta either**. The implemented method-reach contract (`METHOD_REACH`, `cadp/kernel/api.ts`) is a **static method-to-`process_class` table**: it is code, not registry content — `cadp.kernel-config.v1`'s closed schema carries no method-reach key — and it is **kind-blind**: `submit_evidence` reach already exists for the `worker`, `evidence-adapter`, `human-surface`, and `agent-surface` process classes (alongside `workflow` and `deployment-control`). The per-evidence-kind restriction lives one layer down, enforced by the Ingress on every submission: authenticated principal → `identity_registry` entry (exact string; `process_class`; `producer_ref`) → the `adapter_registry` entry for that exact `producer_ref` → `evidence_kinds.includes(kind)` and the registered `source_relation` (TD v0.4 §9.1; `cadp/kernel/ingress.ts`).

The submission contract is therefore explicit: **the actual candidate producer submits `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` directly, as its own registered principal, authenticating as a process class that ALREADY holds `submit_evidence` method reach** — never relayed through the orchestrator, whose relay would seal as `SELF_REPORT` of the orchestrator (§4.1). `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` is **audit-only OPTIONAL evidence** throughout this document — the reference policy requires it for no effect (§4.2 prerequisite matrix) — so what follows describes how a deployment that wishes to **record** candidate-producer provenance registers to do so, never a prerequisite for governing an effect. Such a deployment declares **at most two** registry deltas, and nothing else:

- **(a) `identity_registry`:** exact registration of the producing principal — exact SPIFFE ID or IdP subject, string equality, no patterns — with its `identity_class` (whose `process_class` is one that already holds `submit_evidence` reach) and its exact `producer_ref` (TD v0.4 §5.4; §9.1 S2). An unregistered producer cannot attest; there is no anonymous or pattern-matched attestation path.
- **(b) `adapter_registry`:** the entry for that exact `producer_ref` carries `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` in its `evidence_kinds` **and its `source_relation` is `SELF_REPORT`**. The implemented entry shape is `{ producer_ref, evidence_kinds[], source_relation, produced_at_source }` — exactly **one** `source_relation` per `producer_ref`, covering every kind that entry produces (`REFERENCE_ADAPTERS`, `cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`) — and the Ingress refuses any draft whose `source_relation` differs from the registered value (`SOURCE_RELATION_FORBIDDEN`, `cadp/kernel/ingress.ts`). No wildcard.

**Which producer attests — the canonical entry, and in this generation NOTHING ELSE.** **The actual external producer's canonical K2 identity — its ordinary registered principal and `producer_ref`, whose registered `source_relation` is `SELF_REPORT` — IS the provenance producer**, and that canonical path is the **ONLY supported `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` submission path this generation**. No dedicated registry entry is needed, and none may be created: the producer's canonical `adapter_registry` entry simply carries `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` in its `evidence_kinds`, and delta (a) is already satisfied by the producer's existing registration. This is not a fallback-avoiding convenience — a producer attesting to its own composition *is* self-report by construction, so the canonical entry is the semantically correct shape.

**Where the producer's canonical `source_relation` is anything else, `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` audit submission is UNSUPPORTED in this generation — no identity alias, no dedicated attester, no duplicate-principal registration.** Because `source_relation` is per-`producer_ref` and *not* per-evidence-kind, a principal whose canonical producer entry carries a different relation — e.g. `reviewer:codex`, registered `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` for `REVIEW` (`REFERENCE_ADAPTERS`, `cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`) — cannot simply gain the kind: adding `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` to that entry either refuses at ingress (the draft's `SELF_REPORT` ≠ the registered `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION`; `SOURCE_RELATION_FORBIDDEN`, `cadp/kernel/ingress.ts`) or, if the entry's relation were flipped to match, silently corrupts the relation of every other kind that entry produces. The prior draft's dedicated-attester escape — a separate provenance principal + `producer_ref` pair attesting *for* the execution producer — is **withdrawn as self-conflicting**, on three independent grounds. (i) **It contradicts the claim's own definition.** `claim.producer_refs[]` is (§4.2) the complete set of refs that ACTUALLY produced candidate content, while the `SELF_REPORT` ingress membership rule (§4.2 (d)) requires the submitting stamped ref to be a member of its own claim — so a dedicated, non-producing attester would be forced into a producer set it does not belong to, falsifying the record in exactly the dimension the kind exists to record. (ii) **The binding it depended on has no field to live in.** The `cadp.kernel-config.v1` `identity_registry` and `adapter_registry` entry shapes carry **no execution-producer-to-provenance-attester key** (`KernelConfig`, `cadp/kernel/policyBundle.ts`), and no implemented seam reads one. Stated precisely, so this is not itself an unenforced claim: `validateKernelConfig` closes the key set at the **`data.cadp` top level** only, and does not reject unknown keys *inside* registry entries — so a bundle could carry such a key and activate, and the kernel would simply **ignore** it. An inert, unread key is not a declared binding; the rule's "declare the binding in the registries" had nothing to bind with. (iii) **The implemented identity resolution offers no role selection.** `identity_registry.find((e) => e.principal === principal)` (`identityEntry`, `cadp/kernel/policyState.ts`) resolves an authenticated principal to one row and the Ingress stamps `producer_ref` from that row (TD v0.4 §9.1 S2); an authenticated principal has no way to say *which* of its roles it is speaking in. Therefore: **a deployment whose candidate producer's canonical relation is not `SELF_REPORT` simply has no `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` submission path in this generation.** Should one ever be needed, it is a **future Authority Plane registry contract** — per-evidence-kind `source_relation`, or an explicit attester-binding field, each with its own schema, authority, and provenance — defined **there**, out of scope for this document. The cost is **nil for governance**: the machine-merge path is already closed independently (`HUMAN_DECISION`, §4.2 (c)), so the unsupported case forgoes only *optional audit-tier self-attestation*, and the candidate remains **fully governable** — review, verification, gate-file routing, fail-closed evaluation, and Human merge do not depend on `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` at all. **That "fully governable" claim is a LIVENESS claim, and it holds only because the kind is audit-only OPTIONAL: no effect's admission requires it.** The reference policy's per-effect prerequisite matrix (§4.2) requires `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` for **no** effect — not for `GIT_PUSH`, not for `PR_CREATE`, not for `PR_MERGE` — so a producer with no submission path still has `GIT_PUSH` admissible, `PR_CREATE` reachable, and the `HUMAN_DECISION` merge path reachable. Were the kind required for any effect, this section's UNSUPPORTED verdict would make that effect permanently inadmissible for exactly the producers it applies to, and the promised Human merge would be unreachable — a liveness contradiction. It is not, and no rule in this document may reintroduce one.

**One principal, one row — an ASSUMED property, not an enforced one.** Since `identityEntry` resolves by `.find()`, a principal duplicated across two rows would make resolution `.find()`-**order-dependent**. One-principal-one-row is therefore an **assumed property of the current registries that no validator enforces**: `validateKernelConfig` (`cadp/kernel/policyBundle.ts`) checks principal and `producer_ref` string *exactness* (no glob/pattern characters) and per-entry shape, and checks **no uniqueness** of `principal` or `producer_ref` across rows — `[latent v0.4 hardening: registry uniqueness validation is an Authority Plane contract item, not enforced by validateKernelConfig today]`. It is stated here as an honest description of the seam, not as an enforced guarantee; and with the dedicated path removed above, **nothing in THIS document depends on enforcing it** — no rule stated here is load-bearing on duplicate rows being rejected, since the canonical path stamps whichever single row `.find()` returns for the authenticated producer.

Both deltas are **ordinary policy-bundle changes, gate-controlled like any other registry change** (TD v0.4 §9.4): they ride a `POLICY_ACTIVATE`, never a runtime toggle. **Boundary note:** only a deployment that needs to open `submit_evidence` to a process class that does NOT already hold it would touch the static caller contract — and that is a code-level Authority Plane change, outside `POLICY_ACTIVATE`, out of scope here. This surface remains safe for untrusted callers for exactly the reason TD v0.4 §12 states: no method grants authority — a sealed request still needs evidence, an evaluator decision under the active policy, and the PEP's fresh recheck (Spec v0.5 §4.3 steps 5–7). `requester_ref` is always stamped from the authenticated principal, never accepted from the body (TD v0.4 §9.1 S3).

### 3.3 Effect identity — Platform-sealed, orchestrator-retry-convergent

`effect_id` is allocated by Platform effect ingress before any non-trivial external call; a caller-proposed correlation string is never identity authority (Spec v0.5 K3). Concretely for external proposers:

- The proposer calls `allocate_effect_id` with a versioned canonical tuple (TD v0.4 §7.4 discipline: canonical JSON, closed `purpose` vocabulary, no raw concatenation), schema `cadp.allocation-key.external.v1`: `{ schema, repo_id, candidate_base_sha, candidate_sha, purpose }`. `candidate_base_sha` (§3.4 vocabulary: the exact base revision the candidate was built and reviewed against) is an exact immutable identity component alongside repository identity and `candidate_sha` — without it, two submissions whose candidates sit on materially different base revisions could share an `effect_id`, contradicting K3's binding of one `effect_id` to one `request_digest` over exact material. The Ingress scopes the derived key by the **stamped** requester principal — two principals proposing the same candidate get distinct effect identities; a retry by the same principal converges on the same `effect_id`, so an orchestrator that re-runs a step never creates a second logical effect. (This replaces the internal tuple's `work_run_ref + step_ordinal` scoping, which presumes run-profile enrollment — §5 refuses that here.)
- **Allocation-to-first-seal binding (closes the pre-K3 window).** K3's one-`effect_id`-one-`request_digest` conflict rule presupposes an *existing* sealed `request_digest`; on the first `seal_effect_request` for an allocated `effect_id` there is nothing yet to conflict with, so K3 alone would not stop a first seal naming different material than the allocation — or a first seal by a different principal than the allocator. This document therefore states the **observable cross-plane contract, and only that**: (a) an allocation MUST be scoped to the stamped requester principal and MUST be durable across restart; (b) the FIRST `seal_effect_request` for an `effect_id` MUST verify the allocation binding — stamped principal, `repo_id`, `candidate_base_sha`, `candidate_sha`, and the closed purpose-to-`operation_kind` correspondence (purpose `git-push` ↔ `operation_kind` `GIT_PUSH`, `pr-create` ↔ `PR_CREATE`, `pr-merge` ↔ `PR_MERGE`, the purpose literals coming from the policy bundle's closed `allocation_purposes` vocabulary — `cadp.kernel-config.v1`, TD v0.4 §5.4 — never invented by the caller) — **atomically with respect to the allocation**, so that a mismatch is REFUSED deterministically BEFORE any K3 record exists, including under concurrent first-seal attempts, never degenerating into a post-hoc K3 conflict whose semantics vary by implementation. On refusal, no `EffectRequestV1` is sealed and no effect is performed. The binding is explicitly **not** a new constitutional record — no K8 is created. **The storage schema, transaction, and locking that realize this contract are owned by the Authority Plane TD**, to which this document delegates them (per its own Non-scope; §2). Measured seam, marked as an Authority Plane TD work item: today `allocate_effect_id` does not pass the authenticated principal to the Ingress — `cadp/kernel/api.ts` passes the principal only for `seal_effect_request` and `submit_evidence` — so requester-scoped allocation is not yet realized at that seam. Once the first sealed `request_digest` exists, the ordinary K3 conflict rule remains the guard for everything after it — same material converges, different material conflicts and performs no effect (Spec v0.5 K3). The binding closes the pre-K3 window; K3 governs everything after it.
- A Conductor run identifier is doubly disqualified as an identity component: constitutionally, because K3 forbids caller identity authority; and factually, because P0 measured that `CONDUCTOR_RUN_ID` is not exported to script-step environments despite the documentation saying it is. It may travel as opaque diagnostic content inside `source_ref`/claim payloads; it never keys anything.

### 3.4 The three governed effects and their material bindings

All three re-use the v0.4 GitHub adapter contract of TD v0.4 §6.4 **byte-for-byte** — same materials, same idempotency/precondition rules, same COMMITTED / NO_EFFECT_CONFIRMED proofs. What is new is only who seals the request.

**Vocabulary split — two different "bases".** `candidate_base_sha` is the immutable provenance/input binding: it records which base revision the candidate was built and reviewed against, and it is what the §3.3 allocation tuple and the sealed `work_bindings` carry. `base_ref` is the mutable PR/merge target branch at the target. No existing adapter proves that `base_ref`'s current revision equals `candidate_base_sha` at effect time: `PR_CREATE`'s `base_ref` is a mutable branch that can move between seal and call, and the GitHub merge API's `sha` precondition is a CAS on the PR HEAD, never on the base branch.

| Effect | Material (TD v0.4 §6.4) | External-candidate binding rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GIT_PUSH` | `{repo_id, ref = refs/heads/cadp/candidate/<candidate_sha>, new_sha = candidate_sha, expected_old_sha = 0000…, bundle_cas_key}` | The proposer `put_blob`s a `git bundle` of the candidate; the PEP verifies the bundle reproduces **exactly** `candidate_sha` before pushing (TD v0.4 §6.6, §8.1). An external bundle is exactly as untrusted as a worker bundle was; the same verification makes it safe. The sealed request also binds `candidate_base_sha` as exact immutable input (rule below); the PEP's ancestry check runs against exactly that admitted `candidate_base_sha` (TD v0.4 §6.4) — bundle ancestry against the admitted base is target-state-independent and enforceable, so this effect's base discipline is complete as stated. |
| `PR_CREATE` | `{repo_id, base_ref, head_ref = refs/heads/cadp/candidate/<head_sha>, head_sha, title_cas_key, body_cas_key}` | `head_sha = candidate_sha`; write-once candidate ref shape enforced at admission (TD v0.4 §4.4 #14). The sealed request also binds `candidate_base_sha` as provenance (rule below). `base_ref` is the mutable target branch; this effect does not prove what revision it names at call time. |
| `PR_MERGE` | `{repo_id, pr_number, expected_head_sha, merge_method}` | `expected_head_sha = candidate_sha`; the target-native CAS rejects **head** drift only — GitHub's `sha` precondition is on the PR HEAD, never on the base branch. The sealed request also binds `candidate_base_sha` as provenance (rule below). |

For each of the three effects, `candidate_base_sha` and repository identity enter the sealed `EffectRequestV1` alongside `candidate_sha` as exact immutable bindings: `work_bindings` carries them as exact input identity + revision, covered by `request_digest` (Spec v0.5 K3), and equal by the §3.3 allocation-to-first-seal binding to the allocated values. This is the same exact-input discipline TD v0.4 §8.3 applies to the candidate subject in the development composition — equality over K4-bound sealed facts, decided by the evaluator; inequality = requirement unsatisfied, no K6 — extended to the base revision that composition already pins (worker workspace materialized at exactly `candidate_base_sha`, bundle ancestry checked against the admitted base; TD v0.4 §8.1, §6.4). What this binding proves is **provenance, not target state**: policy and reviewers evaluate against the pinned base the candidate was built and reviewed on; the binding does not — and no measured adapter can — pin the target branch's current revision at effect time. A policy that cares about the target's *current* base takes a fresh target observation (`base_ref` → current sha) into K4 as evidence and applies its drift rule there, evaluator-side (Spec v0.5 §2.4). Atomic exact-base-at-merge enforcement is a target adapter contract gap: `[UNMEASURED — probe required: no GitHub-native atomic base-branch CAS exists in the measured v0.4 adapter contract]`. Any ancestry rule (candidate descends from `candidate_base_sha`) is development-composition semantics per Spec v0.5 §7, evaluated by the evaluator over K4-bound facts — never new kernel vocabulary.

### 3.5 Re-homed unchanged

- **Broker push/PR actuation:** the credential-less bounded broker and PEP-held GitHub credential (TD v0.4 §4, §18.2) actuate; the proposer never touches a governed-target credential (§6).
- **Gate-file routing:** `touchesGateMachinery` (`cadp/product/gateFiles.ts`) applies to external candidates identically — a candidate touching kernel/policy/surface/conformance/constitutional-document paths routes to a `HUMAN_DECISION`, never a delegated agent merge.
- **Agent vs HUMAN merge decision:** the TD v0.4 §12 r9 `agent-surface` contract carries over — `AGENT_DECISION` satisfies only the merge gate, only for producers named in `delegated_merge_producers`, formed by a fresh isolated reviewer, honestly attributed. For an external candidate the carried-over r9 predicate is, and claims only, **proposer/reviewer separation**: a delegate that is, or shares an identity class with, the proposing `requester_ref` is refused. Proposer/reviewer separation is not implementer independence. Where the active policy additionally requires **implementer/reviewer independence**, the predicate is the implemented reference predicate `independent_product` (`cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`), evaluated against the separation basis of §4.2 — which **always includes the stamped `producer_ref` of every gate-tier `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelope the gate consumes, regardless of that envelope's `source_relation` (`SELF_REPORT` | `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` | `TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`) and regardless of its `integrity` (`UNATTESTED` | `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE` | `SIGNED_ATTESTATION`) (§4.2 attester-in-basis), so the party that authored the producer-set claim can never be the party that claim clears** — and extended by observed authorship only in a deployment that declares the §4.2 git-identity alias binding contract: the reviewer's/delegate's `producer_ref` must not be a member of that basis, **AND for every `p` in `producer_refs[]`, `identity_registry[p].identity_class.product != identity_registry[reviewer_producer_ref].identity_class.product`**. The comparison is on the **`product` component**, not whole-object `identity_class` inequality: a delegate sharing the implementer's `product` under a different `account` passes whole-object inequality and is DENYed by the implemented product rule — that same-`product`/different-`account` shape is exactly the self-merge the live self-approval fix closed. Proposer/reviewer separation is retained on top, never replaced. When the basis is absent, or its `producer_refs[]` set is empty, the independence requirement is unsatisfied and no K6 is created (Spec v0.5 §2.5) — an empty producer set proves no independence (`count(implementer_refs) > 0`, `referencePolicy.ts`; TD v0.4 §8.3). And the basis must rest on a source that proves **set completeness**, which no provenance label does: per §4.2, **delegated `AGENT_DECISION` merge of an external candidate is UNAVAILABLE in the reference deployment — the merge decision for an external candidate is `HUMAN_DECISION`, full stop** — because no measured evidence source constructs the exhaustive producer set for a candidate. The predicate above is stated for the deployment that later measures such a source; today it decides nothing for external-candidate merge, because that path is closed before the predicate is reached.

## 4. Evidence submission

### 4.1 Ingress and provenance honesty

External evidence enters as `EvidenceEnvelopeV1` through `submit_evidence`, under the full TD v0.4 §9.1 ingress discipline: `producer_ref` stamped from the authenticated principal; `provenance.integrity` set by the Ingress; `source_relation` checked against the policy-bound adapter registry. The consequence for orchestrator-relayed evidence is mechanical: **when an orchestrator submits a review it ran (the P0 case: a codex review executed inside the Conductor loop), the envelope's producer is the orchestrator's principal and its relation is at most `SELF_REPORT` of the orchestrator** — unless the platform can independently verify the actual producer (a signature the Ingress verifies against `data.cadp.attestation_keys`, sealing integrity `SIGNED_ATTESTATION` under the true producer, or the producer submitting directly as its own registered principal). Self-report is never promoted (Spec v0.5 §4.5); an honest `SELF_REPORT` review is recorded, and simply does not satisfy a gate requiring independence.

### 4.2 What the reference policy accepts for external candidates

The candidate-sha subject-equality rules of TD v0.4 §8.3 (C41 leg 1) apply unchanged — they are composition semantics now (Spec v0.5 §2.4, §7): a required `REVIEW`/`VERIFICATION` envelope satisfies the requirement only if its bound `repo_id + candidate_sha` equals the exact candidate the sealed material names; inequality is requirement-unsatisfied, no K6.

**Candidate-producer provenance (`CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE`).** Proving reviewer ≠ proposer does not prove reviewer ≠ actual implementer: in the Conductor case the proposer is principal C while the actual coder is some agent X, and a reviewer R with R ≠ C proves nothing about R vs X. Spec v0.5's independence generalization requires separation from the producer(s) of the exact subject/material being approved, not from the requester. External candidates therefore carry actual candidate-producer provenance as an **ordinary evidence kind** — no new kernel primitive, and **audit-only OPTIONAL: the reference policy requires it for no effect** (prerequisite matrix below). It is not orchestrator telemetry either, because it is sealed, subject-bound, attributable K2 evidence that a gate *could* consume in the future deployment of (b): a `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` `EvidenceEnvelopeV1` whose subject binds `{ repo_id, candidate_sha }` exactly and whose claim binds `producer_refs[]` — the **complete** set of exact `producer_ref` values that produced any of the candidate's content, never "a" producer. The claim speaks the **`producer_ref` identity domain**, deliberately: K2 seals `producer_ref` — the Ingress resolves the authenticated principal through `identity_registry` and stamps `identity.producer_ref` into the envelope; Spec v0.5 K2 carries no principal field — and the §8.4 predicates and reviewer envelopes all speak `producer_ref`, so a set of principals would force an un-contracted `producer_ref`-to-principal reverse mapping whose 1:1 uniqueness no kernel config guarantees. An orchestrator-relayed claim of who produced the candidate is `SELF_REPORT` **of the orchestrator** and does not establish the producer set — the same rule §4.1 applies to reviews. The authorized submitter exists only by declaration, and in this generation by exactly one declaration: the actual candidate producer submits this kind **directly, as its own registered principal, through its canonical registration whose `source_relation` is `SELF_REPORT`** — the only supported path (§3.2; where the producer's canonical relation is anything else, submission is UNSUPPORTED here, never routed through an alias or a dedicated attester) — under the §3.2 registry deltas: exact `identity_registry` registration of the producing principal, and the `adapter_registry` entry for its exact `producer_ref` carrying the kind in `evidence_kinds`. **The reference policy requires the kind for no effect**, and today no registered `producer_ref` carries it in `adapter_registry.evidence_kinds` at all (`REFERENCE_ADAPTERS`, `cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`) — consistently, since an unrequired kind needs no producer. A **hypothetical mis-authored** policy that nonetheless *required* `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` while no registered `producer_ref` carried the kind would be the unsatisfiable configuration — no authorized caller could produce the required evidence, so the effect would be permanently inadmissible — and the reference policy's authoring tests reject exactly that shape (general rule below). That is a rejected authoring error, **not the reference posture**.

**Assurance decision — PROVENANCE STRENGTH AND SET COMPLETENESS ARE INDEPENDENT PROPERTIES.** The prior draft's error, corrected here normatively: a provenance label grades **who observed or signed the claim**; an independence gate needs, additionally and separately, **whether the claim names EVERY producer**. These are orthogonal axes and neither implies the other. `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` (a `source_relation`) says the claim was made by a party other than its subject; integrity `SIGNED_ATTESTATION` says the claim reached the platform untampered under a verified key. **Neither says anything about the EXHAUSTIVENESS of the claimed producer set.** The counterexample is decisive and general: the actual producers are P1 and P2; an independent observer O — registered `source_relation` `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION`, or submitting under verified integrity `SIGNED_ATTESTATION` — attests `producer_refs = [P1]`, honestly believing that is all it saw; the separation basis is then `[P1, O]` (attester-in-basis included); reviewer P2 is in neither leg and **passes**. P2 co-produced the candidate it just cleared. Strong provenance did not help, because the omission was in the *set*, not in the *observation*. Spec v0.5 §4.3 requires separation from the **ACTUAL producers of the exact subject/material**, which is a statement about the set. Therefore, for an external candidate:

- **(a) Audit tier — accepted, and bounded (unchanged).** `adapter_registry` carries exactly one `source_relation` per `producer_ref` (§3.2), so `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` submitted directly by its registered producer — through the producer's canonical `SELF_REPORT` entry, the only supported path (§3.2) — seals as `SELF_REPORT` with integrity `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE`. That tier is accepted and sealed, and it is the record of claimed producers: recorded, subject-bound to the exact `{ repo_id, candidate_sha }`, accountable to a stamped `producer_ref` (Spec v0.5 K2). Any gate may cite it as audit evidence. An independence gate may not rest on it. Attributability is post-hoc accountability, not prevention. **And the tier is OPTIONAL, not merely non-sufficient: the reference policy requires this kind for no effect** (prerequisite matrix below), so its absence — including its permanent absence for a producer §3.2 leaves without a submission path — blocks no admission.
- **(b) Gate tier — requires a source whose CONTRACT constructs the exhaustive set.** Machine (`AGENT_DECISION`) merge of an external candidate requires an assurance source that **provably constructs the exhaustive producer set for the exact candidate** — not a source that merely observed or signed a claim about it. The required shape is **mechanical composition**, with no party asserting completeness: `candidate_sha` ← the complete set of execution outputs composing it ← platform-observed or attested producer refs for each of those outputs. Completeness is then a property of the composition chain, not of anyone's honesty or thoroughness. **The only candidate source with that shape is the Execution Plane `output_artifact_digest` binding chain** (Spec v0.5 §6; §4.3 execution-provenance strengthening — compared against the sealed material's artifact, never inferred from K4 inclusion). Its availability is `[UNMEASURED — probe required: the Execution Plane capture probe]`. No `source_relation` value and no `integrity` value is a substitute: a strongly-labelled but non-exhaustive claim is exactly the counterexample above.
- **(c) THEREFORE — delegated machine merge of an external candidate is UNAVAILABLE today; the decision is `HUMAN_DECISION`, full stop.** Since no completeness-proving source is measured, **no tier-(b) source exists in the reference deployment**, and so **the merge decision for an external candidate is `HUMAN_DECISION`** (TD v0.4 §9.3 path A). This is the **reference-policy default posture**, stated as a standing design position and not as a temporary apology: `delegated_merge_producers` is `[]` in the reference parameters today (`cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`), and `external_candidate_provenance_producers[]` (consumption rule (a) below) is specified here with an **empty default** — no designation exists in the reference bundle, which is the same closed posture reached by the parameter's default rather than by any runtime condition. The closure is not a gap awaiting a workaround. It is revisable **only** when a completeness-proving source in the (b) shape is measured — a stronger provenance label alone never reopens it. Failing closed to a Human is the design, not a degradation.
- **(d) Self-omitting-attester refusal at ingress — NECESSARY, NOT SUFFICIENT, AND NOT THE GATE'S GUARD.** The submitting envelope's stamped `producer_ref` MUST be a member of its own `claim.producer_refs[]`; an attester that excludes itself from the set it attests is **refused at ingress** (alongside the empty-set rule below — a self-excluding attester and an empty set are the same failure at different sizes). This ingress rule binds `SELF_REPORT` submissions, where it remains necessary: a self-reporting producer omitting itself is making a false claim and the record must not carry it. It is **retained at ingress for claim honesty and is now subsumed, for gate purposes, by the attester-in-basis rule** stated under *Separation basis* below — which excludes a self-omitting attester regardless of `source_relation` (`SELF_REPORT` | `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` | `TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`) and regardless of `integrity` (`UNATTESTED` | `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE` | `SIGNED_ATTESTATION`) — including submissions under a verified signature, and including any attester path a future Authority Plane registry contract might one day open (§3.2), none of which this ingress rule would reach. In this generation the only supported submission path is the canonical `SELF_REPORT` one (§3.2), on which this ingress rule bites directly; the gate rule below is what keeps the guarantee true if that ever changes. Marked explicitly **necessary-but-not-sufficient**: it closes only the self-excluding self-reporter and does **NOT** close third-party co-producer omission — P1 attesting `[P1]` while P2 co-produced satisfies this rule, and for machine merge that case is closed not by any provenance tier but by (c): the machine path does not exist.

**Complete-set contract.** A single-producer field would reopen, at the external boundary, the self-approval hole TD v0.4 §8.3 closed internally by collecting **all** `BACKEND_EXECUTION` producers into `implementer_refs`: a candidate co-produced by P1 and P2, attested naming only P1, would let P2 review it and pass a reviewer-≠-named-producer check. The claim therefore binds the complete producer set, and the attesting principal **attests completeness**: an attestation that omits a producer is a false attestation, attributable to that principal exactly as any authenticated/signed K2 evidence is attributable to its stamped `producer_ref` (Spec v0.5 K2; TD v0.4 §9.1). Stated honestly: the platform cannot *detect* an omitted co-producer from the attestation alone — the K2 integrity model makes omission attributable, not impossible. An **empty** `producer_refs[]` fails closed: it satisfies no independence requirement, because a vacuous universal quantifier is not a proof of independence (the TD v0.4 §8.3 discipline: `count(implementer_refs) > 0`). Identity classes of `producer_refs[]` members are derived through the active policy's identity registry from each `producer_ref` (TD v0.4 §9.1 S2), never read from the claim.

**Separation basis.** Every implementer/reviewer independence predicate in §3.5 and this section evaluates against the **full** `producer_refs[]` set carried by a **completeness-proving gate-tier** source (tier (b) above — none measured today, so for machine merge the path is closed by (c) before this predicate is reached), in the `producer_ref` domain end to end, and the predicate is exactly the implemented reference predicate `independent_product` (`cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`): the reviewer's/delegate's `producer_ref` must not be a member of `producer_refs[]`, **AND for every `p` in `producer_refs[]`, `identity_registry[p].identity_class.product != identity_registry[reviewer_producer_ref].identity_class.product`**. The comparison is on the **`product` component** of `identity_class`, never the whole `identity_class` object: a reviewer sharing an implementer's `product` while differing in `vendor`, `account`, or `process_class` passes whole-object inequality and is **DENYed** by the implemented product rule — the same-`product`/different-`account` self-merge shape the live self-approval fix closed. Identity classes are derived through the active registry from each `producer_ref` (TD v0.4 §9.1 S2), never read from the claim. Proposer/reviewer separation (§3.5) is retained on top, never replaced. When the source is absent, or its `producer_refs[]` is empty, or the only available source is the audit tier, or no completeness-proving source is declared at all, the requirement is unsatisfied and no K6 is created (Spec v0.5 §2.5).

**ATTESTER-IN-BASIS (normative).** The separation basis is **not** the attested set alone. For every implementer/reviewer independence predicate in §3.5 and this section, the basis ALWAYS includes, in addition to the attested `producer_refs[]`, the **stamped `producer_ref` of every gate-tier `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelope the gate consumes for the subject** (the policy-designated envelopes of consumption rule (a) below) — **REGARDLESS of that envelope's `source_relation`** (`SELF_REPORT` | `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` | `TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`) **and regardless of its `integrity`** (`UNATTESTED` | `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE` | `SIGNED_ATTESTATION`). These are the two separate K2 axes (Spec v0.5 K2 field list); neither list is a subset of the other, and `SIGNED_ATTESTATION` is an `integrity` value, never a `source_relation`. The rationale is structural: whoever authored the producer-set claim controls the very basis the independence predicate evaluates over, so **the claim's author may never be the party whose independence that claim establishes**. Two consequences, both intended. First, an attester that omitted itself from the set it attests **is still excluded by construction**: it cannot launder its own provenance out of the basis by routing the claim in under verified integrity `SIGNED_ATTESTATION`, under `source_relation` `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION`, or through any attester path a future Authority Plane registry contract might open (§3.2 — no such path exists this generation), because its stamped ref enters the basis whether or not the claim names it — which is exactly what the `SELF_REPORT`-only ingress rule (d) failed to reach. Second, an honest third-party attester (an independent observer that produced none of the candidate) is simply excluded from reviewing **this one candidate** — which costs nothing, since a different reviewer can review it; independence is a property of the reviewer chosen, not a scarce right. The identity-class leg applies to the attester **exactly as to attested members**: the reviewer's/delegate's `producer_ref` must not equal the attester's, **AND** `identity_registry[attester].identity_class.product != identity_registry[reviewer_producer_ref].identity_class.product` — the same `referencePolicy.ts` product-inequality form, not whole-object inequality. The attester ref enters the basis in the `producer_ref` domain, stamped by the Ingress from the authenticated principal (TD v0.4 §9.1), never read from the claim, so no claim payload can suppress it. Attester-in-basis **extends** the basis; it never rescues a deficient one — an envelope whose attested `producer_refs[]` is empty still fails closed by the rule above, because a basis consisting only of the attester proves nothing about separation from the actual producers.

**Consumption rule for multiple or conflicting provenance envelopes (observable cross-plane contract).** K2 is append-only, so one candidate can accumulate several `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelopes with different `producer_refs[]` sets; and K4 carries caller-influenced `evidence_refs[]`, so an unfavorable envelope could be omitted and a favorable one presented. Three rules, in order:

- **(a) The gate-tier designation is a policy-bundle fact, and its DEFAULT IS EMPTY.** The policy parameter is **`external_candidate_provenance_producers[]`** — the designated **gate-tier** provenance `producer_ref`s for external candidates — activated like any other registry/parameter change (TD v0.4 §9.4). Its **default is EMPTY**, and an **empty designation means no gate-tier provenance exists**, which under the assurance decision above means the machine-merge path is closed and the decision is `HUMAN_DECISION` — the default posture, reached by the parameter default rather than by any runtime condition. A deployment that has measured a completeness-proving source (tier (b)) is the only one that ever populates it. Envelopes from non-designated producers are **not independence-gate inputs**: they can neither satisfy the gate nor DENY it.
- **(b) Assembly completeness is the Platform's obligation — for AUDIT, over all envelopes.** Admission-input assembly for an external-candidate effect MUST include **EVERY** sealed `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelope bound to the exact `{ repo_id, candidate_sha }` subject — never a caller-chosen subset. Completeness is owed at assembly, by the Platform, not by the caller's `evidence_refs[]`. This document states the observable contract only; the storage and query mechanics that realize it are **delegated to the Authority Plane TD** — the same delegation pattern §3.3 uses for the allocation-to-first-seal binding.
- **(c) Conflict comparison is SCOPED TO THE DESIGNATED PRODUCERS, and fails closed within that scope.** Assembly scope and gate scope are deliberately different, and the difference resolves the contradiction a uniform rule would create (a non-designated envelope DENYing a gate it is declared not to feed): **the Platform assembles ALL provenance envelopes for audit completeness, but conflict comparison for the independence gate is performed ONLY across the policy-designated gate-tier provenance envelopes** (`external_candidate_provenance_producers[]`, (a)). Within that designated scope, if more than one envelope is present for the subject and their claim digests are not identical, the independence requirement is **unsatisfied**: no picking, no union, no latest-wins. Sealed history has no "latest" authority (Spec v0.5 §2.6). Identical-digest duplicates converge harmlessly and count as one. A **non-designated** envelope — however many, however conflicting — remains sealed audit evidence and **changes no admission decision**: it can never DENY the gate and never satisfy it.

**Observed authorship (PRODUCT DIAGNOSTICS ONLY absent a declared alias contract — not a K2 evidence item, not a gate input).** The PEP already walks `candidate_base_sha..candidate_sha` when it verifies that the `GIT_PUSH` bundle reproduces exactly `candidate_sha` (§3.4; TD v0.4 §6.6, §8.1), and the author/committer identity strings it observes in that walk are useful to operators. But until a deployment declares a git-identity alias binding contract, those observed strings are **product diagnostics only** — surfaced in logs and operator tooling, never sealed as K2 evidence and never consumed by any gate. A real K2 evidence kind for observed authorship would need its own claim schema, subject binding, producer registration, and seal path — none of which this document owns. The gap is structural: the TD v0.4 `identity_registry` is an exact-string match over SPIFFE IDs / IdP subjects with `identity_class` (§9.1 S2, §5.4); git Author/Committer strings (name + email) are neither, and no git-identity-to-registered-principal alias binding contract exists, so a mapping "through the policy identity registry" is not deterministically evaluable, and the evaluator's separation basis is the attested `producer_refs[]` set **extended only by ATTESTER-IN-BASIS above** (the stamped `producer_ref` of every consumed `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelope) — observed authorship adds nothing to it. `[UNMEASURED — probe required: no git-identity-to-principal alias binding exists in the v0.4 registry contract]`. A deployment that declares the alias contract (alias → registered principal/`producer_ref`, with that binding's own authority and provenance defined) thereby **defines that evidence kind and its registry entries in its own policy/Authority documents** — and only then do the following apply: the use stays one-directional, and stated honestly — commit authorship is producer-asserted content, anyone can commit under any name, so an observed identity string is **never identity proof**; the observed identities, mapped through the declared alias binding, only *extend* the separation basis, so an unreported co-producer visible in the authorship trail still blocks their own review even though the attestation omitted them. A co-producer whose authorship trail is forged or absent is not caught by this extension — that residual is covered only by the complete-set attestation accountability above, and the document marks it as residual rather than closed. Ordering (alias-contract deployments only): the observation comes into existence at `GIT_PUSH` bundle verification, so per this section's prerequisite matrix it can gate `PR_CREATE` and `PR_MERGE` but never `GIT_PUSH` itself, whose pre-push independence rests on the attested set as extended by ATTESTER-IN-BASIS, and on nothing else.

**Per-effect evidence prerequisite matrix (normative).** Evidence can gate an effect only if it can exist before that effect: a GitHub Actions verification exists only after the candidate ref is pushed, so it cannot gate `GIT_PUSH`; a GitHub-native PR review exists only after the PR exists, so it cannot gate `PR_CREATE`. The reference policy gates per effect:

| Effect | Evidence the reference policy requires |
|---|---|
| `GIT_PUSH` | Only evidence obtainable pre-push: any policy-required pre-push `REVIEW` by a registered principal over the submitted bundle/candidate content. **`CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` is NOT required here, nor for any other effect.** When available it is sealed **audit-only OPTIONAL** evidence (§4.2 (a)), and is **not required for per-effect governance or for the `HUMAN_DECISION` merge path**. It becomes gate-relevant only in a future deployment that has measured a completeness-proving tier-(b) source (§4.2 (b)) and populated `external_candidate_provenance_producers[]` (§4.2 (a) consumption rule) — never in the reference posture, whose designation is empty. |
| `PR_CREATE` | May additionally require: the `GIT_PUSH` outcome `COMMITTED`, and a `PRESENT` `VERIFICATION` from `verifier:github-actions` bound to the exact pushed `candidate_sha` with `claim.conclusion == "success"`, `TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`, source-authoritative `produced_at` (TD v0.4 §18.3 fail-closed projection, unchanged; this producer observes the pushed ref at the target, so it is independent of whoever made the candidate by construction). |
| `PR_MERGE` | May additionally require: PR-created facts; a GitHub-native review read via API (subject = `commit_id`, compared by the evaluator) or a platform review of the pushed ref, satisfying the implementer/reviewer independence predicate above (an orchestrator-relayed review only under the §4.1 verification paths — as bare relay it is `SELF_REPORT` and does not satisfy); and `HUMAN_DECISION` (TD v0.4 §9.3 path A) or delegated `AGENT_DECISION` per §3.5. |

General rule — a **REFERENCE-POLICY AUTHORING INVARIANT, not a kernel or activation contract**: no machine-readable effect-to-required-evidence or evidence-to-prerequisite dependency graph exists in `cadp.kernel-config.v1`, and policy is Rego, so no current contract can statically decide satisfiability of an arbitrary policy at `POLICY_ACTIVATE` time — the kernel and `POLICY_ACTIVATE` make NO general satisfiability judgment. The invariant is enforced by the reference policy's own unit/conformance tests: no required evidence for effect E has E itself (or a later effect) as its producing precondition, and every required evidence kind has at least one registered `producer_ref` carrying it in `adapter_registry.evidence_kinds` (§3.2). Honest safety note: an unsatisfiable policy is still fail-closed — it admits nothing — so the invariant protects liveness, not safety. If activation-time refusal is ever wanted, it requires machine-readable prerequisite metadata defined in the Authority Plane TD — out of scope here.

### 4.3 Fail-closed default

Absent, stale, subject-mismatched, or `UNKNOWN` required evidence admits nothing (Spec v0.5 §2.5, §4.5). There is no "external candidates get a lighter gate" mode: an external candidate passes the same evaluator, the same fresh recheck, and the same gate-file routing as an internal one, or it does not pass.

## 5. Explicit non-enrollment in the run profile

### 5.1 Measured facts (P0, run `24919dde`, 2026-09-07)

Conductor's run receipts are local-file grade: an `events.jsonl` under `TMPDIR`, keyed by an 8-hex `run_id`; the run record is **deleted on normal completion**; a checkpoint is written only on failure by default.

### 5.2 The refusal

Run-scope validity grades by the K7 outcome of a `WORK_START` effect (Spec v0.5 §5.2): `COMMITTED` requires a target-authoritative receipt binding the exact effect (the measured v0.4 rule: a describe-read returning a memo matching `cadp_effect_id` and `cadp_args_digest` — TD v0.4 §6.4, C34). A TMPDIR file deleted on success, keyed by a local 8-hex identifier, cannot be that receipt: there is no target-authoritative `WORK_START` outcome to grade a Conductor run scope by K7, and after a normal completion there is no receipt at all. **Therefore the reference deployment enrolls no Conductor principal in the run profile. Conductor runs claim per-effect Kernel Conformance only — never the run profile.** This is a measured refusal, not a policy preference, and it is complete governance, not degraded governance: per-effect Kernel Conformance is the whole constitutional guarantee (Spec v0.5 §5).

Non-enrollment is symmetric and policy-bound (Spec v0.5 §5.1): the active policy simply does not name any Conductor principal among the enrolled `requester_ref`s, and the reference policy reserves run-scope binding for enrolled principals — a non-enrolled principal **cannot acquire run-profile semantics by presenting a `work_run_ref`**. What non-enrollment costs is stated, not hidden: no `MAX_EFFECTS_IN_WORK_RUN`, no `WORK_STEP` chain requirements, no bound-stop semantics for these runs.

### 5.3 Revisit conditions

Enrollment could be revisited only on new measurement: `[UNMEASURED — probe required: a target-authoritative run receipt from the orchestrator — durable across normal completion, correlatable to a Platform-sealed WORK_START effect_id by a target-returned binding (memo-equivalent), and readable by the Reconciler after restart. Absent all three properties, K7 grading of a Conductor run scope remains impossible and non-enrollment stands.]`

## 6. Credential custody at the client (measured)

P0 measured that a Conductor `working_dir` is **not a sandbox**, and that `claude-agent-sdk` loads the working directory's settings as a trust boundary. The consequence is the Spec v0.5 §2.3 rule with no softening: **no governed-target credential may exist anywhere an orchestrator's working directory or settings can reach.** Credential custody stays entirely PEP/broker-side (TD v0.4 §4.1, §18.2: the GitHub credential is PEP-held; the broker is credential-less toward the kernel; workers cannot `admit_and_dispatch` — matrix reach notwithstanding, the worker class cannot even seal requests). The only CADP/governed-effect credential an effect-proposer environment holds is its own Kernel API principal identity, which grants no effect authority by §3.2. A deployment that places a governed-target token in a Conductor working directory has left Kernel Conformance for every effect that token can reach, whatever this document says.

## 7. Conformance controls (falsification additions)

In the Spec v0.5 §9 spirit — proven by attempting the violation:

1. **Replay convergence:** the same external principal re-calling `allocate_effect_id` with the same `cadp.allocation-key.external.v1` tuple receives the same `effect_id`; the target shows one push, one PR (counted at the target, TD v0.4 §13 method).
2. **Identity-squatting refusal:** a different principal submitting the same tuple receives a different `effect_id` and cannot conflict the first principal's request.
3. **Relayed-review honesty:** an orchestrator-relayed review seals as `SELF_REPORT` of the orchestrator principal and a gate requiring independent review DENYs; the same review submitted by the reviewer's own registered principal (or attested per §4.1) admits.
4. **Subject inequality:** review/verification bound to candidate A does not admit an effect naming candidate B (C41 leg 1 rerun against the external client).
5. **Opt-in refusal:** a non-enrolled principal's request carrying a fabricated `work_run_ref` is refused (Spec v0.5 §9.2 negative control, external edition).
6. **Gate-file guard:** an external candidate touching a `GATE_PATH_RULES` path cannot be merged by `AGENT_DECISION`.
7. **First-seal candidate binding:** an `effect_id` allocated for candidate A whose first `seal_effect_request` names candidate B is REFUSED — no `EffectRequestV1` sealed, no effect (§3.3 allocation-to-first-seal binding).
8. **First-seal base binding:** an `effect_id` allocated with `candidate_base_sha` X whose first seal names base Y is REFUSED.
9. **First-seal purpose binding:** an `effect_id` allocated with purpose `pr-create` whose first seal names `operation_kind` `PR_MERGE` is REFUSED (§3.3 closed `allocation_purposes` correspondence).
10. **First-seal principal binding:** principal B first-sealing an `effect_id` allocated to principal A is REFUSED.
11. **Unsatisfiable-authoring refusal (reference-policy tests, not activation):** the reference policy bundle's own authoring/conformance tests reject a requirement that is cyclic — e.g. requiring, for `GIT_PUSH`, the `verifier:github-actions` `VERIFICATION` of the pushed candidate, evidence whose producing precondition is the `GIT_PUSH` itself — and a requirement whose evidence kind no registered `producer_ref` carries in `adapter_registry.evidence_kinds` (§4.2 general rule). The control tests those authoring tests; it does NOT claim an activation-time refusal by the deployment — `POLICY_ACTIVATE` makes no satisfiability judgment.
12. **Producer-set self-review refusal (both legs of the implemented predicate):** leg 1 — a reviewer or merge delegate whose `producer_ref` is a member of the candidate's `producer_refs[]` (any member, not only the first listed) is DENYed. Leg 2 — a reviewer or delegate **absent** from `producer_refs[]` whose `identity_class.product` equals that of any member is equally DENYed; the control constructs the same-`product`/different-`account` delegate, which whole-object `identity_class` inequality would wrongly admit (`independent_product`, `cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`; §4.2 separation basis).
13. **Empty-producer-set refusal:** a `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelope whose `producer_refs[]` is empty satisfies no independence requirement; the gate DENYs exactly as if the evidence were absent (§4.2 complete-set contract; TD v0.4 §8.3 count rule).
14. **Observed-authorship scoping:** in a deployment whose policy declares the §4.2 git-identity alias binding contract, a reviewer absent from the attested `producer_refs[]` but matching, through the declared alias binding, an author/committer identity observed in the PEP's `GIT_PUSH` bundle walk is refused — the alias-extended member enters the basis and is compared by the same `identity_class.product` inequality rule (`independent_product`, `cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`), so a reviewer merely sharing the extended member's `product` is DENYed too; in a deployment WITHOUT the alias contract, the control verifies the converse — that **no K2 evidence and no gate input exists from authorship at all**: observed author/committer strings appear only as product diagnostics, no `EvidenceEnvelopeV1` of any kind is sealed from them, and no admission decision changes when they are present, absent, or altered (§4.2 observed authorship, diagnostics-only).
15. **Audit provenance denies the delegated machine merge while no completeness-proving source is declared — SCOPED TO THE SUPPORTED SURFACE.** The control is stated in three legs, because §3.2 makes the canonical `SELF_REPORT` path the ONLY SUPPORTED `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` submission path this generation, so a strong-label envelope cannot be constructed on the supported surface to be tested. **Leg 1 (the sealable path — runnable now):** on the canonical `SELF_REPORT` path, an external candidate carrying sealed **audit** `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` (`SELF_REPORT` + `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE`, §4.2 (a)) with a delegated `AGENT_DECISION` merge attempted is **DENYed into the `HUMAN_DECISION` path**, while no completeness-proving source is declared and `external_candidate_provenance_producers[]` is **empty** — the envelope is sealed and readable as audit evidence and satisfies no independence gate. The control constructs the substantive case within that surface: producer P1 self-attesting `producer_refs[] = [P1]` for a candidate actually co-produced by P1 and P2, with P2 as the merge delegate, and asserts the DENY (§4.2 (a)–(c)). **Leg 2 (why the strong-label case does not arise on the supported surface — runnable now, and stated at its TRUE enforcement layer):** the bound is a **registry/authoring** one, **not** a kernel kind-to-relation rule, and the control must not claim otherwise. What the Ingress actually enforces is (i) `evidence_kinds.includes(kind)` — in the reference registries **no** `adapter_registry` entry carries `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` at all (`REFERENCE_ADAPTERS`, `cadp/deployment/referencePolicy.ts`), so every submission refuses `EVIDENCE_KIND_FORBIDDEN` before relation is ever compared — and (ii) `adapter.source_relation !== draft.source_relation` → `SOURCE_RELATION_FORBIDDEN` (`cadp/kernel/ingress.ts`), which fires **only on MISMATCH**, in either direction: a producer registered `SELF_REPORT` submitting an `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION`/`TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`-labelled provenance draft is refused, and a producer registered non-`SELF_REPORT` submitting a `SELF_REPORT`-labelled one is refused. The control runs exactly those two refusals and claims nothing beyond them. Stated honestly, so this is not an unenforced guarantee: a bundle that registered a `producer_ref` carrying `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` under a non-`SELF_REPORT` relation would seal a matching strong-label envelope, and **no implemented kernel check prevents it** — §3.2 declares that registry shape UNSUPPORTED this generation, so the control's runnable assertion over the **active bundle** is the checkable registry predicate: **no activated `adapter_registry` entry carries `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` under a relation other than `SELF_REPORT`** — an authoring invariant of the reference posture, in the same sense as §3.2's one-principal-one-row property, not a kernel refusal. And the bound is not load-bearing on the gate: were such an out-of-contract bundle activated anyway, leg 1's mechanism still DENYs machine merge unchanged — `external_candidate_provenance_producers[]` is empty, so the envelope confers no gate tier under any label (§4.2 (a) consumption rule, (c)). **Leg 3 (future guard — EXPLICITLY NOT RUNNABLE THIS GENERATION):** when a future Authority Plane contract opens additional submission paths (§3.2), the leg-1 machine-merge DENY is re-run for **every** `source_relation` (`SELF_REPORT` | `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` | `TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`) × `integrity` (`UNATTESTED` | `AUTHENTICATED_SOURCE` | `SIGNED_ATTESTATION`) combination that contract makes sealable, asserting that **no label combination reopens machine merge absent a measured completeness-proving source** (§4.2 (b)) — strength and completeness being independent axes. Leg 3 is stated as a standing guard on that future contract and is asserted of no deployment today.
16. **Self-omitting attester — BOTH PATHS closed:** leg 1 (ingress, `SELF_REPORT`) — a `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` submission whose stamped `producer_ref` is not a member of its own `claim.producer_refs[]` is **refused at ingress**; no envelope is sealed (§4.2 (d)). Leg 2 (gate, EVERY OTHER AXIS COMBINATION THE DEPLOYMENT CAN PRODUCE) — the same self-omission carried in by a path leg 1 never reaches, i.e. an envelope under `source_relation` `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` or `TARGET_AUTHORITY_OBSERVATION`, or under verified `integrity` `SIGNED_ATTESTATION` whose signature the Ingress accepts, sealing successfully with the attester's own ref absent from `claim.producer_refs[]`: when that attester then attempts the review or delegated merge of the candidate it attested, the gate **DENYs** — its stamped `producer_ref` is in the separation basis by §4.2 attester-in-basis, so both the non-membership leg and the `identity_class.product` leg fail against it. The control runs leg 2 across **every** `source_relation` × `integrity` combination the deployment can produce, not only `SELF_REPORT`, and asserts the DENY does not depend on the claim's contents. Scope note, stated so the control is not read as evidence that an unsupported path exists: in **this** generation the only supported `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` submission path is the canonical `SELF_REPORT` one (§3.2), so a conforming reference deployment can produce no leg-2 envelope at all and leg 2 runs **vacuously** — it is a standing guard for any future Authority Plane registry contract that widens the submission path, and the control asserts exactly that emptiness at its true layer — **no activated `adapter_registry` entry carries the kind under a non-`SELF_REPORT` relation** (the registry/authoring predicate of control 15 leg 2, not a kernel kind-to-relation refusal) — rather than manufacturing one. Residual: third-party omission where the omitted co-producer is neither attester nor reviewer is **MOOT for machine merge** — closed not by this control but by the unavailability of the delegated machine path (control 15, §4.2 (c)) — and remains an honest **audit-tier** residual for the recorded evidence: a sealed audit envelope may name an incomplete producer set, and the platform detects that no more than it ever did.
17. **Conflicting-provenance DENY is scoped to designated producers:** two sealed `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelopes for one `{ repo_id, candidate_sha }` **both from producers named in `external_candidate_provenance_producers[]`** whose claim digests differ DENY the independence gate — the control asserts no picking, no union, and no latest-wins (neither envelope's set is used); identical-digest duplicates admit unchanged. Second leg, asserting the scope: a conflicting envelope from a **non-designated** producer **changes no admission decision** — the control runs the same admission with and without that envelope present and asserts the identical outcome, so a non-designated envelope can neither DENY the gate nor satisfy it (§4.2 consumption rule (a), (c)).
18. **Assembly completeness is the ASSEMBLER's obligation; the gate DENY is DEFENSE IN DEPTH, never a substitute for it:** the assembler MUST include **every** sealed `CANDIDATE_PROVENANCE` envelope bound to the exact `{ repo_id, candidate_sha }` subject, or **refuse the assembly outright** — those are the only two conformant behaviours, and the control asserts the deployment exhibits one of them when handed a subject with an omittable sealed envelope. The evaluator's DENY on incomplete input is **defense in depth** and is **never an alternative implementation of assembly completeness**: a deployment whose assembler **silently proceeds on a caller-chosen subset FAILS this control even if the gate DENYs** on that subset, because the DENY is contingent on what the gate happens to require while the assembly obligation is unconditional (§4.2 consumption rule (b)). Separately, the control asserts that omitting a **non-designated** envelope — equally non-conformant as assembly — changes no admission decision (§4.2 consumption rule (c)).
19. **Attester-reviews-own-attestation DENY (honest third party) — PREMISE: a measured completeness-proving source; NOT runnable in the reference deployment:** this control's premise is a deployment that has measured a **tier-(b) completeness-proving** source (§4.2 (b): the Execution Plane `output_artifact_digest` binding chain, `[UNMEASURED — probe required: the Execution Plane capture probe]`), named that source's producer in `external_candidate_provenance_producers[]`, **and — because that producer attests about candidates it did not itself produce — obtained a submission path for it from the future Authority Plane registry contract of §3.2, which does not exist in this generation.** The premise is thus doubly future-scoped, and the control is stated for the deployment that has both, never as a shape realizable here. **No provenance label confers gate tier**: an ordinary `INDEPENDENT_OBSERVATION` producer, or one submitting under verified `integrity` `SIGNED_ATTESTATION`, is not designable as gate-tier by its axis values (§4.2 (b) — strength and completeness are independent axes), so in the reference deployment — empty designation, no measured source — this control is **not runnable at all** and its situation is governed instead by control 15's DENY. Where the premise holds: the designated attester produced none of the candidate and attests **correctly** — claiming exactly `producer_refs[] = [P1]`, itself legitimately absent because it produced nothing — then attempts to review or merge that same candidate, and is **DENYed**: its own stamped `producer_ref` is in the basis by §4.2 attester-in-basis, regardless of the claim being true, the envelope being policy-designated, and regardless of the envelope's `source_relation` and `integrity` values. The control also asserts the stated cost is exactly the intended one and no more: a **different** reviewer, independent of both the attester and `P1`, **admits** on that same evidence — the exclusion is per-candidate, never a lockout of the attesting product. That admit leg is asserted **only** under the measured-source premise and claims nothing about any deployment lacking one, where the merge decision is `HUMAN_DECISION` by §4.2 (c) and any independence requirement is unsatisfied by the *Separation basis* rule. Third leg: a reviewer sharing only the attester's `identity_class.product` under a different `account` is DENYed too (the attester's identity-class leg, same `independent_product` form as controls 12/14).
